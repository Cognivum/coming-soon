About Cognivum

Cognivum Academic Press is run by academics, for academics. We support editorial independence and transparent publishing workflows designed around research quality.

Our journals follow a Diamond Open Access model, enabling both free publishing and free access for readers, while maintaining high ethical and editorial standards.
