About Cognivum

Cognivum Academic Press is an international academic publishing initiative dedicated to promoting scientific excellence, research integrity, and global knowledge exchange.

Our journals provide a platform for original research, theoretical contributions, evidence-based practice, and interdisciplinary scholarship across business, marketing, analytics, and related fields.

We believe that scientific knowledge should be accessible, transparent, and impactful. Through rigorous editorial standards and independent peer review, we aim to contribute to the advancement of scholarly communication worldwide.

Why Publish with Cognivum Academic Press?

  • Run by Academics, for Academics

    Cognivum Academic Press is led by active researchers and academics who understand the challenges, expectations, and realities of scholarly publishing. Our editorial decisions are guided by academic merit, integrity, and scientific contribution.

  • Diamond Open Access (No APCs)

    We believe that knowledge should be accessible without financial barriers. Authors are not charged submission or publication fees, and all published articles remain freely available to readers worldwide.

  • Rigorous and Supportive Peer Review

    Our journals employ a double-blind peer review process that ensures fairness, quality, and constructive feedback. We aim not only to evaluate research but also to help authors improve their work.

  • High Scientific and Editorial Quality

    We are committed to maintaining high standards in editorial assessment, peer review, publication ethics, and academic rigor to ensure the quality and credibility of published research.

  • Continuous Publication Model

    Accepted articles are published promptly once they complete the editorial and production process, ensuring faster dissemination of scientific knowledge.

  • Authors Retain Copyright (CC BY)

    Authors maintain copyright ownership of their work while allowing broad dissemination and reuse through Creative Commons licensing.
